## Origin story supplied by the user — 29 October 2026

The user provided an oral recollection in Hindi/Hinglish and explicitly asked for it to be added to the website. The English “Before the first flight” section adapts that recollection: plastic gliders; packing visiting foreign pilots’ wings and getting small chances to handle them; inspiration from acro displays during Bir’s 2015 World Cup; a family prohibition on learning to fly; joining AntiGravity after Class 12; a 5 a.m. office routine explained at home as running, with afternoons described as playing with friends and evening sessions as more running; showing the first-flight video at home, the family’s shock, and the objections subsequently going quiet. The recollection is not assigned to a named pilot, because the user did not identify its narrator. No inference of all three pilots sharing an identical childhood or family history is made. The hero heading was changed to **ACRO IS NOT A CRIME**, correcting the obvious spelling slip in the requested phrase.
