## Design research

The design is original. References informed the emphasis on athlete identity, flight films, maneuver exploration, and a strong outdoor sporting character:

- [Acro World Tour](https://acroworldtour.com/): pilots, teams, tricks and the culture of acro competition.
- [Pál Takáts](https://paltakats.com/): athlete story, films and flying experiences.
- [Fly The Earth](https://flytheearth.com/): acro community, flying equipment and ambassadors.
- [Horacio Llorens air shows](https://www.horaciollorens.com/en/air-show/): performance storytelling.
- [Red Bull Air Race design project](https://www.alexandergustavsson.com/red-bull-air-race): action sports presentation and visual hierarchy.
- [justACRO videos](https://justacro.com/video): film-led acro community presentation.

No page layouts, copy, logos or source code were copied from these references.
